# 00 — Overview: PulseHQ Case Study

## ⚡ Executive Summary
**PulseHQ** is an AI-powered squad telemetry and predictive workload management platform designed for Engineering Managers, Technical Product Leads, and Squad Directors. It transforms fragmented team status data into an actionable command dashboard featuring one-click workload rebalancing, dynamic multi-theme navigation, interactive SVG analytics, and an integrated AI assistant.
